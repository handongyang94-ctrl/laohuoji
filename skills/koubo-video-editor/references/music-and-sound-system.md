# Music and Sound System

## Hard Gate

For a finished video, sound must be designed. There are only four valid decisions:

- `music_bed`: low-volume background music supports speech.
- `music_led`: music structure drives visual rhythm.
- `section_music`: different music or energy by section.
- `intentional_silence`: silence/room tone is stronger than music.

If the agent simply forgets music, the video is not finished.

## Music Jobs From The Course

Music can:

- preload emotion before the image or speech enters
- express inner world or theme
- create atmosphere
- separate sections
- support rhythm and speed
- lift a climax
- land the ending

Do not treat music as wallpaper.

## Music Selection Logic

Choose keywords from:

- project type: talking-head, product, ad, travel, story, teaching
- material: factory, store, road, product detail, city, nature, office
- script tone: warning, premium, warm, intense, clean, technical, emotional
- speaker voice: casual, formal, business, deep, fast, calm
- section function: hook, proof, montage, climax, ending

Examples:

- trust-heavy talking-head: low, clean, minimal, no lyrics, low distraction
- product/commercial: business, clean, premium, technology, energetic depending on brand
- travel/visual: airy, spacious, regional, atmospheric
- fast ad: high-energy, pulse, beat, impact
- emotional story: restrained rise, theme, ambience

## Speech-Led Rules

- Speech intelligibility wins.
- Prefer instrumental or minimal beds.
- Duck music under key judgments, numbers, warnings, and CTAs.
- Avoid lyrics under Chinese speech unless the lyrics are deliberately part of the concept.
- Keep the opening music low enough that the first useful word is not masked.
- If music makes the video feel fake, choose quieter ambience or silence.

## Music-Led Rules

Before placing visuals:

1. Listen or analyze the whole track.
2. Mark intro, groove, lift, drop, climax, release, ending.
3. Assign script/material sections to music sections.
4. Cut near beats, not mechanically on every beat.
5. Use fast-slow contrast; do not make every shot the same length.
6. Use the strongest product/proof/action shots at lifts or climaxes.

## Sound Bridge Rules

- Use J-cut when the next section should emotionally arrive before the picture changes.
- Use L-cut when a visual jump needs smoothing.
- Use natural sound when it proves real action: machine, door, product use, crowd, water, vehicle.
- Use SFX only for meaningful text hits, product actions, reveals, transitions, or impacts.
- Do not use SFX as decoration on every caption.

## Practical Volume Defaults

These are starting points, not laws:

- talking-head music bed: very low, around 5-12 percent perceived level
- montage music: lead level, but duck under voiceover
- SFX: short and lower than speech unless it is a deliberate impact
- natural sound: audible only when it proves action or atmosphere

Always verify by listening or ASR/loudness evidence where possible.
